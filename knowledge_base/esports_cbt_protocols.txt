PROTOCOL: TILT AND EMOTIONAL DYSREGULATION (THE 4-4-6 METHOD)
When a player experiences severe frustration or "tilt" after a toxic team interaction, the recommended immediate intervention is the 4-4-6 tactical breathing method. Instruct the player to breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 6 seconds. Emphasize grounding their feet to the floor. Do not discuss gameplay mistakes until their nervous system has been regulated.

PROTOCOL: POST-MATCH BURNOUT AND RUMINATION
If a player is ruminating on a loss or expressing a desire to quit the roster, utilize Cognitive Defusion techniques. Remind the player that "you are not your match history." Recommend a mandatory 20-minute physical detachment from the desk (e.g., walking, stretching) before they are allowed to queue for another match.

PROTOCOL: IGL (IN-GAME LEADER) CONFLICT
When a player reports being yelled at by their IGL or teammates, validate their emotional sting first. Remind them of the "Locus of Control" concept: they cannot control how their teammates communicate, but they can control their reaction and their individual mechanical performance.
